# Champion class icons (Codex) — 1 sheet, 6 icons

Same pixel-art look as the item/ability icons (crisp hard pixels, black outline, bright readable colors, no anti-aliasing). Tech / hacker sci-fi MOBA.
Each icon is ONE emblem (badge-style symbol, strong silhouette, ~80% of the cell), centered, NO text, NO borders, NO background square (the game paints one). Flat PURE MAGENTA #FF00FF background everywhere, no other magenta in the icons.

## Sheet format (strict — a script slices it)
`art/raw/classes/sheet.png`, exactly 768 x 512 px: grid of 3 columns x 2 rows, every cell exactly 256 x 256, row-major.

1. todos — a four-pointed star / four small squares emblem (all classes), cyan and white
2. ai (Poder de IA) — a glowing brain-chip: a brain made of circuit traces with a spark, purple/violet
3. hacker — a hooded figure silhouette with a terminal cursor "> _" in the face area, neon green
4. cyber (Cybersecurity) — a heavy shield with a padlock in the center, steel blue and cyan
5. soporte — a medical cross merged with a wrench (repair + heal), green and white
6. programador — a pair of angle brackets "</>" with a small fist/hammer, orange and yellow
